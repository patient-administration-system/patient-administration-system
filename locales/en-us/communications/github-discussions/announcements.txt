Welcome to Patient Administration System Discussions

Discussions are now open for the Patient Administration System: an open-source, administrative (not clinical) system for hospitals and clinics that handles appointments, reminders and replies, waiting lists, referrals, and transport and virtual-visit requests. This is the place to ask questions, suggest changes, and share what you build with it.

## What this means for you

- You can ask a question without opening an issue.
- You can suggest an idea and get feedback before anyone writes code.
- The maintainers will post news here, such as releases and changes of direction.

## Where we are today

The system is built and tested, but it is **not in use with real patients**. Most of what remains needs people or outside parties: a Clinical Safety Officer, sign-off of the data protection impact assessment, real message carriers, an external penetration test, usability testing with patients and staff, and review of the translations by first-language speakers. The path to production lists every item with an estimate of how far it has got:
https://github.com/patient-administration-system/patient-administration-system/blob/main/governance/path-to-production.md

## Where to go for what

- **Questions** about running or using it: Q&A
- **Suggestions** for features or changes: Ideas

- **Something you built** or tried with it: Show and tell

- **Bugs**: Issues, at https://github.com/patient-administration-system/patient-administration-system/issues
- **Security problems**: not here. Follow the security policy at https://github.com/patient-administration-system/patient-administration-system/blob/main/SECURITY.md
