# Governance

A single-maintainer project. The maintainer decides; the specification ([`spec/`](spec/index.md)) and the task list ([`tasks.md`](tasks.md)) record what was decided and why.

- **Changes** follow the three-part rule (spec, code, test) in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Disagreements** about behaviour are settled by editing the spec in the open, then the code.
- **Safety and privacy** concerns are never closed as "won't fix" without a written reason in the relevant governance document.
- **Becoming a maintainer** is by invitation, after sustained, reviewed contributions.
- **Deploying organisations** own their own clinical safety case, DPIA and assurance. Nothing in this repository substitutes for them.
